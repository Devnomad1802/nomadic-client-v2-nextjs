## GetYourGuide — observed (evidence, mobile 2026-10-28)

- **Search-first hero:** headline "Experiences worth travelling for" with a **large search bar immediately below** as the dominant above-the-fold element, then a horizontal "Top destinations" card rail.
- **GDPR consent modal** on entry ("Let's give you the best experience possible" + Let's go / Change preferences).
- **Takeaway vs Nomadic:** GetYourGuide treats **search as the hero**; Nomadic leads with editorial brand copy and places search deeper. For high-intent discovery, a more prominent above-the-fold search/destination entry could help — **without** abandoning Nomadic's editorial, host-led differentiation.

## 10 transferable patterns worth adopting (general best practice)

1. **Dates/availability on the detail page** (not hidden a step later) — show next departures + "seats left" (addresses UX-004).
2. **Search prominence** for destination/experience intent above the fold, balanced with brand voice.
3. **Review density with real counts** near the title and CTA (Nomadic already shows "5.0 (3)" — keep counts honest).
4. **Sticky, price-bearing booking CTA on mobile** — Nomadic already does this well; keep it.
5. **Faceted filters with result counts** ("Showing 4 trips") — Nomadic has the scaffold; make counts update live per filter.
6. **Clear cancellation/refund visibility** near price (reduce booking anxiety).
7. **Skeletons + reserved media aspect ratios** to avoid perceived-empty heroes (addresses PERF-006).
8. **Trust/safety module** on detail (verified host, secure payment) — Nomadic already has it; keep it consistent.
9. **Single canonical category taxonomy** surfaced consistently (addresses UX-002).
10. **Consent + privacy affordance** where cookies/analytics are used (addresses UX-010).

## 5 patterns to deliberately AVOID

1. **Fabricated urgency / countdowns / "X people viewing"** — conflicts with Nomadic's honest, host-led brand.
2. **Mismatched/deceptive discount badges** (see UX-005) — one truthful figure only.
3. **OTA-style ad/upsell density** — don't bury the host-led story under promo clutter.
4. **Interstitial app-install / newsletter walls** before users can browse.
5. **Over-aggressive personalization cookie walls** that block first browse — keep consent lightweight.

## Differentiators to protect

- **Host-led, people-first narrative** ("find someone worth travelling with") — unique vs transactional OTAs.
- **Budget-conscious, authentic, community positioning** — avoid drifting luxury or generic.
- **Real host profiles + direct messaging** ("Message on Nomadic Townies") — a trust moat most OTAs lack.
